# Code of Conduct

We strive to maintain a respectful, inclusive community. By participating, you agree to:

- Be kind, considerate, and professional in all interactions.
- Assume good intent, seek clarity, and avoid personal attacks.
- Welcome differing viewpoints; critique ideas, not people.
- Respect boundaries and personal space; avoid harassment.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers. Consequences may include warnings, temporary bans, or removal from the project.
